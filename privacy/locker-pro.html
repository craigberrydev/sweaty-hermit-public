Locker Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Summary

Locker Pro is an offline combination-lock practice app. It does not contain advertising, analytics, accounts, social features, or tracking. It does not send personal information or locker combinations to Sweaty Hermit or to third parties.

Developer

Locker Pro is published by Sweaty Hermit. Questions about this privacy policy or the app’s privacy practices may be sent to craigberry.dev@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

A user may enter a three-number locker combination and select practice preferences, such as dial size, turning direction, teaching options, and realistic lock behavior.

This information is used only on the user’s device to provide the lock-practice experience. Locker Pro does not transmit this information to Sweaty Hermit or to any third party.

Data collection and sharing

Locker Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or disclose personal information or user-entered locker combinations.

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics software development kits. It does not create user accounts and does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or other sensitive device permissions.

Local storage and security

The saved locker combination and practice settings are stored locally in Android’s private storage for Locker Pro. They are not stored on Sweaty Hermit’s servers.

Locker Pro excludes its stored information from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Under normal Android security controls, other apps cannot access Locker Pro’s private storage.

No method of local storage can provide absolute security. Someone who can unlock, modify, or compromise the device may be able to access information stored on it. Users should protect their device with an appropriate screen lock and should avoid entering a sensitive combination on a shared or untrusted device.

Retention and deletion

A saved locker combination remains on the device until the user chooses Forget my saved locker code, clears Locker Pro’s app storage through Android settings, or uninstalls the app.

Because Locker Pro does not receive or retain the combination on a server, Sweaty Hermit has no server-side copy to retrieve or delete.

Children’s privacy

Locker Pro is designed to be usable by students, including children. The app does not knowingly collect or transmit personal information from children.

Locker Pro does not contain advertising, behavioral tracking, social features, or purchases, and it does not ask children to provide their name, contact information, location, photograph, voice, or account information.

Third-party services

Locker Pro does not use third-party services to collect, process, or analyze user data. Downloading the app through Google Play and using the Android operating system remain subject to the privacy practices of Google and the user’s device provider.

Changes to this policy

If Locker Pro’s data practices change, this privacy policy and the app’s Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated as appropriate before the changed version is released. The effective date displayed at the top of this page will also be updated.
